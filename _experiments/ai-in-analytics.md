AI can support analytics in several meaningful ways, from auto-summarizing reports to helping users ask natural-language questions about data. The key is to use AI as an augmentation layer rather than a replacement for sound data reasoning.

## Examples of AI in analytics

- generating summaries from dashboards or reports
- interpreting metric changes and explaining anomalies
- helping users derive insights from large datasets
- converting natural-language questions into query logic
- producing narrative explanations for stakeholders

## Strengths

AI can accelerate analysis by reducing time spent on repetitive tasks and making insights more accessible to non-technical users.

## Risks

Without proper validation, AI-generated insights may sound confident but still be incorrect or incomplete. Strong human oversight remains essential.

## Final lesson

The best use of AI in analytics combines technical data skill with careful judgment. It should support decision-making, not replace the analyst's critical thinking.
